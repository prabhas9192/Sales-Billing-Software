A. Customer Management

Add/edit/remove customers.

Store details like name, contact, address, GST/Tax info.

B. Product/Inventory Management

Add products with name, code, price, tax %.

Track stock quantities (optional for basic version).

C. Billing/Invoice Generation

Create new bills with selected products & quantities.

Auto-calculate totals, tax, discounts.

Generate printable PDF invoices.

Optionally save invoices for history.

D. Payment Tracking

Record payments (cash, card, online).

Show unpaid balances.

E. Reports

Daily/weekly/monthly sales summary.

Customer-wise or product-wise sales.

Tax reports (GST or local taxes).
